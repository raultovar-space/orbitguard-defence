ASAT fragmentation Active

A destructive anti-satellite test on a defunct payload near 470 km, like Cosmos 1408 in November 2021. About 1,500 fragments are spread with realistic velocity kicks, then the whole fleet is screened again.

Inspector approach Active

An uncorrelated object appears 90 km behind a geostationary weather satellite on a slightly lower orbit, so it drifts closer every hour.
